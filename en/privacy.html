Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Information Collection and Use

FlashNote does not collect any personally identifiable information. We only use your Apple ID and iCloud account to sync your note data, which is stored in your personal iCloud account. We have no access to your note content.

Data Storage

Your note data is stored and synced through iCloud private database. We do not maintain any separate server-side database. All data is governed by Apple's iCloud Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud: Note data sync and storage
  • CloudKit: iCloud backend database service

Data Security

All data transmitted through iCloud is end-to-end encrypted. We follow Apple's platform standard security practices to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated prominently within the app. By continuing to use FlashNote, you agree to the updated policy.
