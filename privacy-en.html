Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

Information Collection and Use

FlashNote does not collect any personally identifiable information. We only use your Apple ID and iCloud account to sync your notes. All data is stored in your personal iCloud account, and we have no access to the content of your notes.

Data Storage

Your notes are stored and synced via iCloud private database. We do not maintain any separate server-side database. All data is governed by Apple's iCloud Terms of Service and Privacy Policy.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Apple iCloud: Note data sync and storage
  • CloudKit: iCloud backend database service

Data Security

All data transmitted through iCloud is end-to-end encrypted. We follow Apple's platform security best practices to protect your data from unauthorized access.

Children's Privacy

Our service is not intended for children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any material changes will be communicated prominently within the app. Continued use of FlashNote constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
